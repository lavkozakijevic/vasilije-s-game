# Spring cutscene

640×360 layers, drawn at 320×180 and doubled, same rules as the intro and ending.

- **spring_01_sunrise**: `spring_01_sunrise_bg.png`, `spring_01_sunrise_fx.png`. Motion: slow pan up from the fence to the house as the sun rises; chimney smoke and petals drift; birds cross left to right
- **spring_02_living_room**: `spring_02_living_room_bg.png`, `spring_02_living_room_chars.png`, `spring_02_living_room_fx.png`. Motion: slow push in; dust floats in the sunbeam; Vasilije's Zz drift up; Konstantin's stretch can bob 1px
- **spring_03_ruby**: `spring_03_ruby_bg.png`, `spring_03_ruby_chars.png`, `spring_03_ruby_fx.png`. Motion: hold, then a small shake as Ruby calls; the call lines pulse

02 is the living room from the photos: the four cousins waking up (Konstantin stretching, Katarina asleep on the daybed, Vasilije under a blanket on the floor, Dimitrije rubbing his eyes) with toys everywhere. 03 is Ruby in the white doorway in an apron with a wooden spoon, hands on hips, calling the kids.
