# Shared FX

- `fx_dust_jump.png` 32×16 ×4 @16 and `fx_dust_land.png` 32×16 ×4 @16: anchor bottom-center at the hero's feet.
- `fx_splash_water.png` 32×32 ×5 @14: bottom-center on the water surface when anything falls in.
- `fx_hit_spark.png` 16×16 ×3 @18: centered where a projectile hits an enemy.
- `fx_hero_respawn.png` 32×32 ×6 @10: the fire version. Each hero folder has its own `fx_<element>_respawn.png` in its element colors. Play it at the checkpoint, then switch to idle.
